# Roadmap

The order of work on the edge protocol. A new version is announced with the KeyClerk release that speaks it, so an
edge ports once per version.

## Done
- 1.5.0: the edge's trusted time, the ratchet and the desk's time; how a node of a high-availability edge became
  active; relays that tighten and never grant, and the site key's statements.
- 1.4.0: the records head echoed, removal confirmations, the lease on evidence entries, a lease ended early, the
  bastion's declared capabilities and the adapter profile, ceiling hours rounded up.
- 1.3.0: the address leaves the bundle, fetched by request.
- 1.2.x: an edge may wait for its next bundle; references cited where first named.
- 1.1.0: governed roles in the bundle.
- 1.0.0: the first public specification and conformance set.
